Executive Performance Review Copywriting Playbook & Formulae

Comprehensive enterprise taxonomy, active action verb matrices, and defensible review syntax for MNC Leaders.

Raw-to-Executive Phrase Transformer (白話轉 C-Suite 句型)
"Expeditiously resolved critical cloud architecture anomalies while championing peer technical upskilling."
1. Action-Oriented Verbs

Replace passive descriptions with high-impact executive verbs:

  • Execution: Spearheaded, Deployed, Architected, Accelerated.
  • Leadership: Mentored, Galvanized, Championed, Instituted.
  • Strategy: Formulated, Optimized, Aligned, Harmonized.
2. Quantifiable Impact Syntax

Quantify outcomes using concrete enterprise metrics:

  • Cost Reduction: Reduced infrastructure compute overhead by 35%.
  • SLA Uptime: Maintained 99.99% availability during multi-region migration.
  • Team Acceleration: Increased overall sprint velocity by 40%.
3. Defensible Growth Directives

Construct constructive feedback without creating legal HR risks:

  • Focus exclusively on objective business outcomes, not personal traits.
  • Provide clear, actionable pathways for promotion readiness.
  • Maintain STAR structural alignment across all written reviews.
